## Research Assistance & Projects


<details class="course-details" markdown="1">

<summary>
Perfect Withdrawal Rate (PWR) Analysis
<span class="inst-badge">Bayes</span>
</summary>

<div class="course-body" markdown="1">

<div class="badges">
  <span class="meta-badge">Research Project</span>
  <span class="meta-badge">Python</span>
</div>

Supervised by [Prof. Andrew Clare](https://www.bayes.citystgeorges.ac.uk/faculties-and-research/experts/andrew-clare).

Developed a Python framework to compute and simulate the Perfect Withdrawal Rate — the maximum constant annual withdrawal sustainable over a fixed horizon without depleting a portfolio below a target terminal value.

Applied the framework to global equity benchmarks covering the US, UK, Europe, Japan and Emerging Markets, using Monte Carlo simulation, distributional statistics and comparison against the Sharpe ratio.
